Core Mechanic 1 — Calculate Takt Time

Game shows demand.

Example:

Client needs:

20 store interiors
in 10 days

Takt time:

Available time / demand

Example:

480 minutes per day
20 stores in 10 days
Takt = 240 minutes per store

Players set takt.
